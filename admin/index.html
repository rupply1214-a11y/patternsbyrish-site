The free Supabase plan holds 500 MB. Orders and wishlists are tiny (about 1 to 2 KB each), so this lasts for many thousands of orders. At 80% you'll see a warning here; then download a backup in Orders and remove old orders.

Website

When do changes show on the website?

The website checks Etsy and this admin panel every hour and updates itself. New Etsy listings and anything you save here appear within an hour. Orders, download links and coupons work straight away.

        Download links

        How to
        1. Open Google Drive, right-click the pattern's folder (or zip), choose Share, set Anyone with the link, then Copy link.
        2. Paste the link in the box next to that pattern. It saves by itself and shows Saved.
        3. Tap Test to check the link opens. Patterns without a link show first.

            New patterns (website only)

            How to
            1. Tap Add a pattern, fill in the name and price, and add photos (the first photo is the main one).
            2. Paste its Google Drive link at the bottom, so buyers get the files.
            3. Turn on Show on the website and tap Save pattern. It appears on the website within an hour.

            Patterns you sell only here, not on Etsy. They get their own page, show in their categories, search and the cart, just like Etsy patterns.

              Sale & top message

              How to
              1. The yellow strip at the very top of every page shows these messages.
              2. Sale prices and the countdown come from Etsy by themselves; you don't set them here.
              3. Edit or add a message, then tap Save changes.

              Sale prices

              Nothing to set here. The website reads each pattern's sale price, discount and end time straight from Etsy every hour, and again just after midnight India time when your daily sale changes. When a sale ends, the website switches back to the normal price on its own and the yellow bar shows the countdown.
