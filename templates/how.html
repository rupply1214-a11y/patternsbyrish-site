How PDF sewing patterns work

A PDF pattern is the same pattern you'd buy in an envelope, delivered as a file. You print it, tape the pages together and cut out your size.

  1. Buy the pattern on Etsy

    Each pattern on this site has a Buy on Etsy button. Checkout happens on Etsy, so you can use the payment method and account you already have.

  2. Download your files

    The download button appears on your order straight away. Save the files to your computer, or to your phone if you'll print from there.

  3. Print at actual size

    Open the PDF in a free reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. In the print settings choose 100 percent or actual size. Fit to page shrinks the pattern and changes the fit.

  4. Tape the pages together

    Trim one edge of each page, line up the marks, and tape. Work row by row. It usually takes 15 to 30 minutes, with a podcast on.

  5. Cut your size and sew

    Each size has its own line. Follow yours, or trace it onto tracing paper so you can make other sizes later. Then follow the instructions step by step.
